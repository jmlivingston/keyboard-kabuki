# Keyboard Kabuki

An iOS app that gives each keyboard language its own background color, chosen by the user, without ever looking at what they type.

## Language

**Kabuki Keyboard**:
One of our own custom keyboards, covering exactly one language (for example, the French Kabuki Keyboard), drawn in that language's Language Tint.
_Avoid_: custom keyboard, extension, French keyboard (ambiguous with Apple's)

**Language Tint**:
The background color the user has assigned to a Kabuki Keyboard's language.
_Avoid_: theme, keyboard color, background
